## P-01 Definitions

### P-01.1 Benefit Year
The benefit year is the calendar year, from January 1 to December 31. Annual maximums, visit limits and visit thresholds reset at the start of each benefit year.

### P-01.2 Eligible Member
An eligible member is a plan member whose coverage is active on the date of service. Claims for services rendered before coverage start or after coverage termination are not payable.

### P-01.3 Completed Visit
A completed visit is a scheduled appointment at which the practitioner rendered a covered service to the member. Appointments cancelled by the member or the provider, and no-show appointments, are not completed visits. Cancelled and no-show appointments do not count toward visit thresholds, prior authorization thresholds or visit limits.

### P-01.4 Allowed Amount
The allowed amount for a visit is the lesser of the billed amount and the fee schedule maximum per visit for the benefit. Reimbursement is calculated as the reimbursement percentage multiplied by the allowed amount, subject to the remaining annual maximum.

## P-02 Eligibility and Effective Dates

### P-02.1 Coverage Period
Coverage begins on the member coverage start date and ends on the coverage termination date, if any. Eligibility is evaluated against the date of service.

### P-02.2 Policy Effective Date
This policy is effective for services rendered on or after January 1, 2026. Claims with a date of service before the policy effective date are adjudicated under the prior policy and are not payable under this policy.

## P-10 Paramedical Benefit Coverage by Plan

### P-10.1 Plan Coverage Table
NorthStar Bronze covers physiotherapy and chiropractic care. NorthStar Silver covers physiotherapy, chiropractic care and massage therapy. NorthStar Gold covers physiotherapy, chiropractic care, massage therapy and acupuncture. Services not listed for a plan are not covered.

## P-12 Provider Eligibility

### P-12.1 Eligible Practitioners
Paramedical services are payable only when rendered by a practitioner who is registered, in good standing and not suspended in the NorthStar provider registry on the date of service. Claims from suspended or deregistered providers are denied.

## P-14 Physiotherapy

### P-14.1 Physiotherapy Reimbursement
Physiotherapy is reimbursed at 80% of the allowed amount. The fee schedule maximum is $120 per physiotherapy visit.

### P-14.2 Physiotherapy Annual Maximum
The physiotherapy annual maximum is $750 per member per benefit year. Once the annual maximum has been reached, further physiotherapy claims in the same benefit year are not payable.

### P-14.3 Physiotherapy Prior Authorization
No prior authorization is required for the first 10 completed physiotherapy visits in a benefit year. Requirements for physiotherapy visits beyond the tenth completed visit are under review under change request BR-391; until this section is amended, no authorization requirement is enforced for physiotherapy.

## P-15 Chiropractic Care

### P-15.1 Chiropractic Reimbursement and Maximum
Chiropractic care is reimbursed at 80% of the allowed amount, with a fee schedule maximum of $90 per visit and an annual maximum of $500 per member per benefit year. No prior authorization is required.

## P-16 Massage Therapy

### P-16.1 Massage Therapy Reimbursement and Maximum
Massage therapy is reimbursed at 80% of the allowed amount, with a fee schedule maximum of $100 per visit and an annual maximum of $500 per member per benefit year.

### P-16.2 Massage Therapy Visit Limit
Massage therapy is limited to 20 completed visits per member per benefit year.

## P-17 Acupuncture

### P-17.1 Acupuncture Reimbursement and Maximum
Acupuncture is covered under NorthStar Gold only. It is reimbursed at 80% of the allowed amount, with a fee schedule maximum of $90 per visit and an annual maximum of $400 per member per benefit year.

## P-20 Prior Authorization

### P-20.1 Authorization Requests
Where prior authorization is required, the treating provider submits an authorization request through the NorthStar Authorization Service before the service is rendered.

### P-20.2 Authorization Validity
An approved authorization remains valid for the remainder of the benefit year for the authorized benefit, unless the authorization states otherwise.

### P-20.3 Claims Without Required Authorization
A claim for a service that requires prior authorization, submitted without an approved authorization on file, is denied with reason code AUTH_REQUIRED. The claim may be resubmitted once an authorization is approved.

## P-30 Claims Submission

### P-30.1 Duplicate Claims
A claim with the same member, provider, benefit, procedure and date of service as a previously adjudicated claim is a duplicate and is denied with reason code DUPLICATE.

### P-30.2 Timely Filing
Claims must be submitted within 12 months of the date of service.

## P-40 Member Communication

### P-40.1 Notice of Benefit Changes
Material changes to benefit maximums or authorization requirements must be communicated to plan members and providers at least 30 days before the change takes effect.
