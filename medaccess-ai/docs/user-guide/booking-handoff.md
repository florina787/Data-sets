# Booking handoff

A booking handoff gives you the provider's official booking link or phone number together with the opening you selected. MedAccess AI does not book appointments on your behalf and does not send your information to the provider.

Your appointment is confirmed only when the provider confirms it directly with you. MedAccess AI never shows a booking as confirmed unless a verifiable confirmation arrives from an authorized provider integration.

Before preparing a handoff, MedAccess AI asks for your approval. You can reject the request at any time and nothing is prepared.

# Cancelling an existing appointment

MedAccess AI does not cancel appointments. If you secure an earlier appointment, contact the provider of your original appointment yourself to cancel it, so the slot can be offered to another patient.
