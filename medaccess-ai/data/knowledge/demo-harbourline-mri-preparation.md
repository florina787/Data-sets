# Booking instructions (fictional demo facility)

Harbourline Imaging Centre (Demo) accepts the standard imaging requisition. Patients can book through the online booking page or by phone. Bookings are confirmed by the facility by phone.

# Preparation (fictional demo facility)

The facility sends a safety screening questionnaire before every MRI appointment. Arrive 20 minutes early with your requisition and health card. Follow any additional preparation instructions provided by the facility or your clinician.
