# Emergencies

In an emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department.

# Health811

Health811 is a free, confidential Ontario service. You can call 811 to speak with a registered nurse for health advice, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

# Crisis support

If you are thinking about suicide, call or text 988, the Suicide Crisis Helpline, available 24 hours a day across Canada.
