# Requisitions

Diagnostic imaging and laboratory tests listed on MedAccess AI require a requisition from a clinician. A requisition is the form your clinician completes to order the test.

Some facilities accept only their own network-specific requisition form. If a facility does not accept your requisition format, ask your referring clinician whether a new requisition can be issued for that facility.

If you do not have a requisition, contact your referring clinician. MedAccess AI cannot issue or modify requisitions.

# Referrals

Specialist consultations require a referral from a clinician. The specialist clinic usually contacts the patient after receiving the referral.

# Coverage

MedAccess AI does not determine or guarantee provincial health coverage. Facilities marked private-pay may charge fees. Always confirm costs and coverage directly with the provider.
