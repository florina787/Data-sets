# Welcome to VEENA 👋

Thank you for being here. VEENA is a private AI companion that runs on your own computer, with open models,
no account and no subscription. It is young, and what you tell us decides what it becomes.

### Where to post

| | |
| --- | --- |
| 💬 **General Discussion** | How you use VEENA, local AI, open models, anything related. |
| 💡 **Feature Requests** | One idea per post. Upvote the ones you'd use. |
| ✨ **Product Feedback** | What works, what doesn't, what surprised you. |
| 🛟 **Support & Issues** | Installation or usage help. Mark the answer that solved it. |

Prefer to stay private? Use the feedback form on the website: it goes straight to the developer.

### Before you post

* Include your **operating system**, **VEENA version** (About page) and **what you expected**.
* For logs, use **Settings → Diagnostics → Show log**: secrets are redacted. Read it before you paste.
* **Never post** passwords, API keys, private documents, meeting transcripts or screenshots of other people's data.
* **Security problems:** please don't post them here. Report them privately through the
  security policy (`veena-ai/SECURITY.md` in this repository).

### Ground rules

Be kind and specific. Assume good intent. Small local models can be wrong; VEENA shows its sources so you can
check, and that is a fair thing to discuss. Nobody here is owed a feature, and every idea is read.

Welcome. We're glad you're here.

— Florina Regius, VEENA.AI
