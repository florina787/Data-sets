What VEENA does

Answers from your documents

PDF, Word, text, Markdown, CSV and JSON, indexed on your computer. Every answer cites the passage it used, and says so when nothing matches.

Catches made-up facts

Names and figures in an answer are checked against what VEENA was given. Anything not backed is flagged right under the answer.

Meeting notes

Summary, decisions, action items and open questions from a transcript you are authorised to capture. A recording indicator is always visible.

Screen intelligence

Explain code, find errors or summarise a page. Only the screen or window you pick is captured, and detected secrets are masked first.

Interview practice

Generated questions for eight roles with scored feedback, for rehearsal. VEENA is not designed for covert help in live interviews.

Measured agents

Knowledge, context and procedural graphs score every answer with transparent rules, so you can see how much to trust it.

Install in four steps

  1. Download the installer for your computer above.
  2. Install. Windows: run the installer. If SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC", this beta is not yet code-signed: choose More info, check the publisher and file name, then Run anyway. macOS: open the disk image and drag VEENA to Applications.
  3. Open VEENA from the desktop or Start menu icon. Setup checks your computer and, with your permission, downloads the free AI runtime (Ollama, MIT licence) and an AI model. Allow 5 to 20 minutes, mostly downloading.
  4. Ask your first question. Setup says "VEENA is ready." only after the model has really answered on your computer.

Full guide: installation guide · troubleshooting. Check a download against the SHA-256 checksums published with each release.

Your data stays on your computer

  • Conversations, transcripts and documents are stored in a local database. Export or delete them at any time; history is deleted automatically after 30 days by default.
  • AI answers are generated on your computer. VEENA does not send your data to a cloud AI service, and has no telemetry.
  • Raw audio and screenshots are never saved.
  • Internet is used to download the AI runtime and models, and, if you allow it, to check once a day for a newer version number.

AI models

VEENA recommends only models whose licences allow commercial use (Apache 2.0), downloaded through the local runtime. See the model licence audit.

Known limitations

  • This is a beta. Installers are not yet code-signed, so Windows and macOS show a warning on first launch.
  • No build for Intel Macs yet.
  • On a laptop without a GPU, answers take a few seconds and screen analysis can take a minute or more.
  • Small local models make mistakes. VEENA shows its sources and flags unbacked claims so you can judge.
  • Capturing other apps' audio (system audio) works on Windows only.
