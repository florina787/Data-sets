SYNTHETIC POLICY for an independent prototype.

## Content rules
Customer updates state only facts supported by cited records. Do not promise restoration times, credits, compensation, or appointment slots that are not confirmed in a system of record.

## Credits and compensation
Credit eligibility is determined by billing rules and financial authority, never by the support assistant. Tell the customer the request has been noted and how it will be reviewed.

## Sending
In this prototype, customer messages are drafts only and are never sent.
