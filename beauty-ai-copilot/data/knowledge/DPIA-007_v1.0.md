## §1 Residual risks
Residual risk: subgroup labels in synthetic fixtures do not validate any real-world grouping method. A real pilot needs a documented grouping protocol and privacy review before collection.

## §2 Logging
Images and image-derived embeddings must not be written to application logs or sent to language-model providers.
