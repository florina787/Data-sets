DEMO DOCUMENT — fictional consent rules for a prototype. Not legal advice.

## §1 Permitted use
An image may be used for evaluation only when its consent record is granted, lists "evaluation" as a permitted use and its retention date has not passed. Training use needs a separate explicit "model_training" permission.

## §2 Prohibited inference
Systems must not infer identity, race, ethnicity, health conditions or other protected traits from a face. Evaluation group labels must come from an approved protocol, never from speculative demographic inference.

## §3 Deletion
Deletion covers raw images, thumbnails, embeddings, annotations and derived records. Backups expire on their own schedule (35 days in this demo design) and are not edited in place.
