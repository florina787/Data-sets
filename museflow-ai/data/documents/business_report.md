Company: Glimmer Goods (fictional)
Period: Q3 2026
Prepared By: Finance Team (fictional)
Revenue: $3,400,000
Gross Margin: 42%
Customer Growth: 18%

## Summary

Glimmer Goods grew revenue by 12% compared with the previous quarter, driven by its subscription candle line. Gross margin improved to 42% after renegotiating packaging costs. Customer growth reached 18%, the strongest quarter this year.

## Risks

Shipping costs rose 9% in the quarter. A key supplier has signalled possible delays in November. Marketing spend efficiency declined slightly in paid social channels.

## Outlook

Management expects Q4 revenue between $3,800,000 and $4,100,000, supported by holiday demand. The team will prioritise supplier diversification and retention campaigns.
