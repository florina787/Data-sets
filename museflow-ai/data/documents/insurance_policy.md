Coverage Limit: $250,000

## Covered Events

The policy covers sudden and accidental damage to the insured home caused by fire, storm, hail or burst pipes. Personal belongings inside the home are covered up to 30% of the coverage limit. Temporary accommodation is covered for up to 60 days when the home is uninhabitable.

## Exclusions

The policy does not cover gradual wear and tear. Flood damage from rising surface water is excluded unless the optional flood endorsement is purchased. Damage caused intentionally by the policyholder is excluded. Losses while the home is vacant for more than 90 consecutive days are excluded.

## Making a Claim

Claims must be reported within 30 days of discovering the damage. The policyholder should take photos, keep receipts, and prevent further damage where it is safe to do so. A claims adjuster may inspect the property before payment is approved.

## Renewal

The policy renews automatically unless cancelled in writing at least 14 days before the expiry date. Premiums may change at renewal.
