# R. Regulatory & risk intelligence

FLO provides research tooling, not legal advice. **The AI does not make final legal or regulatory determinations.**

* Reference material (`data/documents/`) is synthetic and **illustrative**: it names frameworks such as NI 31-103, the
  client focused reforms, CIRO rules, PIPEDA / Québec Law 25, PCMLTFA and OSFI E-23 only as labelled references.
  Applicability to any specific entity must be determined by Legal and Compliance; the documents say so.
* Workspace features: policy search with citations (permission-aware RAG), regulatory change comparison (section-level
  diff of DOC-003 → DOC-004), control mapping (18 controls ↔ exceptions ↔ reference mapping), exception management with
  audited status changes, evidence collection (event, control, audit trail, related policies), compliance approval of AI
  communications, and an audit report (audit-chain verification, exception and approval statistics, policy decisions).
* Suitability screening uses only approved, versioned rules (`policies/suitability.yaml`) and outputs findings for human
  review; personalized recommendations require compliance approval.
