# Assessment
Before recommending a change, advisors compare the portfolio's annualized volatility with the band for the client's risk profile (Conservative 8%, Moderately Conservative 10%, Balanced 13%, Growth 16%, Aggressive 22%) and check single-issuer concentration against the 10% limit. KYC information older than 730 days must be refreshed first.

# Review
Personalized recommendations generated with AI assistance require compliance approval before delivery to the client.
