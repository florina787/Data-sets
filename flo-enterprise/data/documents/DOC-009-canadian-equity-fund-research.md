# Range overview
The FLO Canadian equity mutual fund range (synthetic) charges management fees between 1.60% and 2.00%. Ten funds were repriced by 15 basis points in November 2025.

# Peer comparison
Lower-cost ETF alternatives charge between 0.15% and 0.55%. Over ten years, a 2.00% fee on CAD 100,000 growing at 6% gross reduces the ending value by roughly 31,000 compared with no fee.

# Suitability note
Product research is general information and not a recommendation for any specific client.
