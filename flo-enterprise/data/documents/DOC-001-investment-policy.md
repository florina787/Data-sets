# Purpose
This synthetic investment policy statement governs discretionary portfolios managed by FLO Investments (a fictional firm).

# Concentration limits
No single issuer may exceed 10% of a discretionary portfolio's market value at month-end. Breaches must be reported to the CIO within two business days and remediated within 30 days unless an exception is approved by the Investment Risk Committee. Enterprise-wide exposure to any single issuer above 4% of total managed portfolio assets triggers a committee review.

# Rebalancing
Portfolios are reviewed monthly. Drift of more than 5 percentage points from target asset-class weights requires a rebalancing proposal. Rebalancing proposals are portfolio modifications and require CIO approval before any instruction is generated.

# Liquidity
Funds must maintain liquidity sufficient to meet stressed redemptions of 10% of AUM within five business days.
