# Decision context
Industry flows are rotating toward low-cost ETFs. Expanding the ETF shelf captures these flows but dilutes the firm's revenue yield because ETF fees are a fraction of mutual fund fees.

# Expected impact
Target ETF AUM of CAD 4.5 billion by September 2026. Management accepts lower average revenue yield in exchange for asset growth and client retention.

# Assumptions
Distribution partners list new ETFs; ETF fee levels remain between 15 and 55 basis points.
