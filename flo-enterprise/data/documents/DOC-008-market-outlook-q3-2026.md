# Summary
Synthetic research view: equity markets recovered from the spring 2025 drawdown. Technology issuers led returns, raising concentration in growth portfolios. Fixed income offered positive carry.

# Risks
Key risks are a renewed equity correction, rising rates that would reduce bond prices, and continued fee compression across actively managed mutual funds as investors favour ETFs.

# Positioning
The research team recommends reviewing single-issuer concentration in growth mandates, particularly exposure to Northgate Technologies, which is widely held across portfolios.
