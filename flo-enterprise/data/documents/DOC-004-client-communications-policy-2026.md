# Approval of communications
Communications to clients that discuss performance or products must be reviewed by Compliance before sending. Communications drafted with AI assistance must be approved by a compliance officer through the approval workflow, and the approved text must be sent without modification.

# Prohibited content
Communications must not promise or guarantee returns, must not present projections as certain, and must include standard risk disclosures. Personalized recommendations require a documented suitability assessment.

# Records
Approved communications and their approval records are retained for seven years.
