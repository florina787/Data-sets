# Important
This is an illustrative internal summary prepared for a technology demonstration. It is not legal text and must not be relied upon. Applicability of any framework to a specific FLO entity must be determined by Legal and Compliance.

# Know your client and suitability
Registrants are generally expected to collect and keep current know-your-client information and to take reasonable steps to ensure that investment actions are suitable for the client and put the client's interest first (see NI 31-103 and the client focused reforms; illustrative reference).

# Conflicts of interest
Material conflicts are to be identified and addressed in the best interest of the client.

# Recordkeeping
Records of client communications and of the basis for recommendations are generally required to be retained (illustrative reference to recordkeeping obligations).

# Dealer members
Where a business is a CIRO dealer member, CIRO rules on suitability, communications and complaints may apply in addition to securities legislation (illustrative).
