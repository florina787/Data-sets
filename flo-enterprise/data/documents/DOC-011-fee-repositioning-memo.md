# Decision
Reduce management fees by 15 basis points on ten Canadian equity mutual funds effective November 2025 (decision D-0001).

# Rationale
Competitive pressure from low-cost ETFs and peer fee surveys. The aim is to slow net redemptions.

# Assumptions
Flow elasticity to fees of 1.5x (not validated); no competitor response within 12 months. Revenue yield dilution is accepted.
