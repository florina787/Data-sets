# Minimization
Only the personal information necessary for a task may be used. Client names, account numbers, SINs, contact details and dates of birth must be tokenized before being sent to any AI model provider or tracing service.

# Rehydration
Tokens may be converted back to original values only for authorized users with a legitimate purpose, and every rehydration is logged.

# Retention and deletion
Token mappings expire after 30 days. Deletion requests are processed by the Privacy Officer through the secure deletion workflow.
