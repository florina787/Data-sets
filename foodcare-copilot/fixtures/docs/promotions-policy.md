# Promotions Policy

## 1. Scope
Applies to every consumer promotion run on Food Beverage Food Care digital storefronts. All content in this document is synthetic demo material.

## 2. Product eligibility
Every promotion must list eligible products by SKU. Brand names or product families are not sufficient, because brand families contain products with different margins and regulatory labels.

## 3. Geographic eligibility
Regional promotions are determined by the shipping address on the order. Browser location, IP address and language settings must not be used to grant or deny an offer.

## 4. Combining offers
Multi-buy promotions (for example buy X get Y) do not combine with discount codes unless the campaign brief explicitly approves stacking.

## 5. Customer limits
Promotions may limit redemptions per customer. The brief must state the scope of the limit. This policy does not define a default scope.

## 6. Timing
Campaign start and end times are set in the local time of the target market, and the brief must name the timezone. Systems must convert local times to UTC explicitly and must handle daylight-saving transitions.

## 7. Stock
"While stock lasts" offers must reserve stock when the order is created. Offers must not be honoured for units that cannot be reserved.
