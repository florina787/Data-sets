# Ontario Campaign Guidelines

## 1. Timezone
Ontario campaigns run on America/Toronto time.

## 2. Weekend campaigns
A "weekend" runs from Saturday 00:00 to Monday 00:00 local time. The brief must give the calendar dates.

## 3. Identity
Customer-limited offers require a signed-in customer account. Anonymous checkouts are not eligible for limited offers.
